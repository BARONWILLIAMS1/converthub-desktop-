# ConvertHub — Emerald Luxe design foundation

- Direction: precision instrument, expressed through quiet Emerald Luxe.
- Modes: light and dark.
- Light palette: ink #10231F, canvas #F4F8F5, emerald #0C9B72, muted #64756D, border #DCEAE2, surfaces #FFFFFF and #E4F2EB.
- Dark palette: deep green #10231F / #18342C with mint accent #42B98F.
- Tight workspace pacing; clear hierarchy; restrained radii, hairline borders, soft shadows.
- Type: Instrument Serif display with DM Sans UI.
- Motion: short tactile transitions, clear progress, reduced-motion support.
- No gradients, decorative illustrations, or noisy category colors.
